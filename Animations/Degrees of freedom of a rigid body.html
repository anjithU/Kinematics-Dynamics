A rigid body has six degrees of freedom

Three translations place it, three rotations orient it. Every constraint you add takes some of those away.

Tick a box to lock that motion (a constraint removes exactly one DOF). Coloured rails and rings in the scene show the motions still allowed. Rotations are applied about the world x, y, z axes in that order, so near 90° on y two rotations can look alike (gimbal lock).
