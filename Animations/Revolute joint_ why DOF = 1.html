Revolute (pin) joint: why DOF = 1

A free rigid body has 6 degrees of freedom. The pin and clevis remove 5 of them. Drag to orbit, then pull the joint apart and swing the link.

Motion of the linkStatusWhy
Slide along xblockedpin bears against the clevis holes
Slide along yblockedsame: the hole holds the pin’s centre
Slide along z (pin axis)blockedthe two ears trap the link’s eye
Rotate about xblockedpin is supported at two points, so it can’t tilt
Rotate about yblockedsame reason
Rotate about z (pin axis)FREEa round pin in a round hole can spin
DOF = 6 − 5 constraints = 1

In planar mechanisms the same joint is 3 − 2 = 1: it only removes the two in-plane translations.
